1. Problem & Q1–Q5 System Architecture

Multi-camera surveillance across blind zones and look-alike distractors requires verifiable handoff decisions. Amidst structures the workflow into five auditable stages:

  • Q1: Filter candidate cameras by user-selected conditions.
  • Q2: Locate matching visual appearances in footage.
  • Q3: Parse structured workbench query selections (non-NL).
  • Q4: Judge frame-level target presence with explicit uncertainty.
  • Q5: Decide camera switching when the target exits the field of view.

STRICT GT ISOLATION Ground Truth, renderer trajectories, visibility, and depth are excluded from inference, ranking, and reconstruction. Every output preserves source frame, clock, run, and SHA-256 provenance.
